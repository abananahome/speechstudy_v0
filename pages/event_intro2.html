Evaluation Session 2 of 2

Original Oratory

Instructions

In this session, you will watch five complete competitive speech performances.

Please evaluate each performance according to your own judgment. There is no required rubric or set of criteria.

Watch each performance in its entirety before continuing.

You will not be asked to rank the performances until you have watched all five.

After viewing the complete set, you will rank the performances relative to one another and explain your evaluations.

Original Oratory is an event in which competitors write and memorize a speech based on a specifc topic or argument.
