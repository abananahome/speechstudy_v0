Evaluation Session 1 of 2

Program Oral Interpretation

Instructions

In this session, you will watch five complete competitive speech performances.

Please evaluate each performance according to your own judgment. There is no required rubric or set of criteria.

Watch each performance in its entirety before continuing.

You will not be asked to rank the performances until you have watched all five.

After viewing the complete set, you will rank the performances relative to one another and explain your evaluations.

Program of Oral Interpretation is an event in which competitors prepare scripts containing selections of prose, poetry, drama, music, etc to create one cohesive piece and argument. This layered style is achieved by splicing different texts together. While POIers hold the black binder, they are not required to read from it, and it is used in tandem with body movement to enhance the argument overall.
